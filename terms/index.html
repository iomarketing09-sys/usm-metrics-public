1. About this app

The Universe Sent Me Metrics App is a private, local analytics tool operated by iO Marketing for the Universe Sent Me brand. It is used only to review performance information for social accounts that the authorized operator controls.

The app does not publish content, edit videos, manage comments, send messages, run advertising campaigns, process payments, or act on behalf of unrelated brands.

2. Authorized use

The app may be connected only to Universe Sent Me social accounts authorized by their owner or administrator. It must not be used to access third-party accounts without authorization.

3. Limited access

The app requests only the permissions needed to read account and video performance data. It does not request permissions to post, edit, delete, comment, message, manage advertising, or transfer funds.

Analytics data is provided by the relevant platform and may be delayed, incomplete, unavailable, or corrected later. Estimated monetization values may change after platform adjustments. The app does not guarantee any performance, reach, revenue, or business outcome.

5. Suspension or revocation

The authorized operator may revoke platform authorization at any time through the relevant platform. The app may be paused or discontinued if access is no longer authorized, an account does not belong to Universe Sent Me, or platform rules change.

6. Contact

For questions about this app, contact iO Marketing through the public contact channel confirmed by the authorized operator.
